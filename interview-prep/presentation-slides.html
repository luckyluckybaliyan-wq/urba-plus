👥 Target Users & Affected Stakeholders
👤 Citizens
Urban residents facing broken roads, garbage, waterlogging — with no easy way to report or track
👷 Municipal Officers
Field crews overwhelmed with duplicate reports, no proof of work, and manual paperwork

🔒
No Audit Trail
Logs tampered, no SLA tracking, no transparency for citizens
⚠️ Limitations of Existing Solutions
❌ MyGov / PG Portal — Manual triage, no AI, no duplicate detection, no proof of repair
❌ FixMyStreet (UK) — Basic photo upload only, no AI verification, English only
❌ All existing apps — Zero platforms verify resolution using AI video analysis
❌ No platform has tamper-proof SHA-256 audit chain or community voting
🏙️ Civic Sentinel — Our Solution
A closed-loop AI verification platform that automates issue classification, eliminates duplicates, verifies repairs using AI video analysis, and ensures full transparency through tamper-proof audit logs — all in one unified platform for citizens, field authorities, and admins.

AI Duplicate Detection

🎥

7 Indian Languages + Karma System
Full UI in EN · HI · MR · TA · TE · GU · BN — citizens earn points, badges & leaderboard rank
