Part 3 — Planned Features: National-Level Upgrades

These 7 features would elevate the project from a strong MVP to a national hackathon winner.

01 · MAP
🗺️ Live Ward Heat Map
Interactive map showing all active issues as colored pins — red = Critical, green = Resolved. Leaflet.js + OpenStreetMap (free). GPS data already exists in Firestore.
⚡ Instant visual wow factor. Turns the app into a real smart-city command centre. Data is already there — just needs to be mapped.
02 · ANALYTICS
📊 SLA Analytics Dashboard
Admin charts: open vs resolved over time, avg resolution time per department, AI verification pass/fail rate, top issue categories. Recharts + existing Firestore data.
⚡ IBM judging requires evidence-based governance. Fills the single biggest current gap. Shows the platform measures and improves itself.
03 · PROOF WALL
📸 Before / After Proof Wall
Public gallery of resolved issues showing original complaint photo side-by-side with authority's after-repair frame. Citizens rate quality (1–5 stars).
⚡ Makes accountability visible. Perfect live demo — show a pothole reported and repaired with photo evidence. Strongly differentiates from any civic app.
04 · SECURITY
🔐 SHA-256 Tamper-Proof Audit Chain
Every audit log entry gets a SHA-256 hash chained to the previous entry (mini blockchain). Admin "Verify Integrity" button checks the chain — shows ✅ or ❌ tampering alert.
⚡ Fulfils the existing synopsis claim. Enterprise-grade auditability critical for government adoption. IBM evaluators will recognise this immediately.
05 · AI CHATBOT
🤖 Gemini Civic Assistant Chatbot
Floating chat widget. Answers: "Status of ticket #CS-2847?", "Potholes near me?", "How to report a water leak?". Gemini with function-calling on live Firestore data.
⚡ Demonstrates Gemini for multi-turn reasoning with live data — not just image classification. No competing team will have this. Maximum AI innovation score.
06 · PREDICTIVE AI
📈 Predictive Hotspot AI
Gemini analysis over 30 days of resolved issues predicts which wards will have the next infrastructure failure. Outputs ranked hotspot list with confidence scores and preventive actions.
⚡ Transforms the app from a reactive complaint box into a predictive governance tool. Exactly what IBM Smarter Cities promotes. Strong differentiator.
07 · PUSH
🔔 Web Push Notifications
Browser push (Service Worker) — citizens get instant mobile alerts on status change without checking email. Free, no third-party cost. Also enables basic offline support.
⚡ India-specific impact — reaches citizens who don't use email regularly. Shows digital inclusion thinking. Service Worker enables offline resilience as a bonus.
